# Existing systems and the gap we target

| System | What it does (as publicly described) | Our angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Ishara AI** (Kenya, ishara.co.ke; IRCAI Global Top 100, 2025) | KSL ⇄ text/speech | Service interactions with robots/kiosks, agentic task completion, explicit error-tolerance research, offline core |
| **Signvrse** (Kenya) | AI sign-language translation with avatars | Robustness + context + service-agent integration as research contributions |
| **SignAll** (hackathon reference) | Speech → text; images of sign gestures | Image generation isn't sign production. We generate pose sequences |
| Signa AI, VocalSign, Hand Talk, SignComm, Kozha | Various text/speech → avatar or sign → text/speech | Mostly other sign languages. We're KSL-specific with Kenyan English/Swahili speech |
| Maseno University AI4KSL and related academic work | KSL datasets and recognition research | We build on their data and add continuous signing in service contexts |

**Don't claim "first KSL translator".** Claim the combination.

Sources: [IRCAI: Ishara AI](https://ircai.org/top100/entry/ishara-ai/) · [MultiLingual: Signvrse](https://multilingual.com/signvrse-ai-translation/) · [Maseno: AI4KSL workshop](https://maseno.ac.ke/stakeholder-workshop-ai4ksl-bridging-language-barrier-using-artificial-intelligence-kenyan-sign)
